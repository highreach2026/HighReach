Simple, Fast & Respectful of Your Time

01

We look past resumes and inspect your actual work—live websites, Figma files, GitHub repos, or motion reels. We respond within 3–5 business days.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Got Questions? We’ve Got Answers

What is High Reach’s work location and hybrid policy?

Our creative studio is headquartered in Bengaluru, Karnataka. We offer a flexible hybrid working model, giving you quiet focused days to execute deeply alongside designated in-person studio days for collaborative brainstorming, design critiques, and team offsites.

What do you look for most in an applicant’s portfolio?

We look for clear critical thinking and intentional craft. Don't worry about showing 20 projects; 2 to 3 well-documented case studies showing the problem, your exact contribution, the decisions you made, and the quantifiable outcome make the biggest impression on our team.

Can I apply if my skills don't match 100% of the requirements?

Yes! We hire for curiosity, taste, and speed of learning over static checklist perfection. If you're driven to build extraordinary work, select "General Application / Other" in the form below and show us what you're proud of.

How fast will I hear back after submitting my application?

We respect your time. Every submission is personally reviewed by our agency founders and discipline leads within 3 to 5 business days, and we ensure every candidate receives clear communication.
